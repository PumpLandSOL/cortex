Documentation

How the CORTEX agentic financial engine works, end to end.

1 · Intent → Plan → Execute

Every interaction follows one loop:

  • Intent — you say what you want in plain language: swap 500 USDC to SOL, alert me when SOL > 200, spend $40 on dinner.
  • Plan — the agent structures it: route, fees, resulting amounts, and validation against your actual balances. Invalid plans can't be executed.
  • Execute — one click confirms. The engine re-validates server-side at current oracle prices, settles, and mints a receipt into your account tape.
The agent never moves capital without your confirm. Read-only intents (analysis, quotes) execute instantly.

2 · Invest — the integrated DEX

Swaps route at live Pyth oracle mid-price across BTC, ETH, SOL, USDC, and $CRTX, with a flat 0.10% network fee. No order books to babysit, no app-switching — quotes, execution, and settlement in one surface.

3 · Analyze

Portfolio analysis marks every holding to the live oracle and flags concentration risk (>60% in one asset), thin stable reserves (<10% USDC), and idle capital. Price alerts watch the oracle continuously; the moment one fires it's logged to your tape and analysis is queued.

4 · Earn

Stake $CRTX into the earn vault at 12% APY, accrued per second. No lockups — unstake returns principal plus rewards instantly.

5 · Spend

The card rail settles purchases from your USDC balance with a 0.1% network fee and credits 2% cashback in $CRTX immediately. Real-world utility meets on-chain capital.

6 · $CRTX

$CRTX is the bloodstream of the system: the earn vault denominates in it, cashback pays in it, and fee routing accrues to it.

7 · Honesty layer

CORTEX is a technology demonstration. All app balances are simulated paper positions — the engine holds no custody, moves no real funds, and issues no cards. Prices are real (Pyth oracle); execution is paper. Nothing on this site is financial advice. $CRTX is a memecoin with no expectation of profit from the efforts of others.
